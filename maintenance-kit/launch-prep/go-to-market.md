# Go-to-Market Plan

> **Version:** PDF v1.0.0 | **Kit:** Maintenance | **Stage:** 6 — Launch Preparation

---

## Overview

A go-to-market (GTM) plan is your systematic answer to: **"How will the right people learn our product exists and decide to try it?"**

Without a GTM plan, even excellent products launch silently — no reviews, no organic discovery, no feedback loop. This document structures your launch into coordinated actions that maximize the chance of a strong opening.

---

## Part 1: GTM Strategy Brief

Fill this in before building the tactical timeline.

```
GTM STRATEGY BRIEF — [Project Name]

PRODUCT POSITIONING
  Product in one sentence: [App Name] is the only [product type] that [key differentiator].
  Target user:             [User description — be specific]
  Primary job-to-be-done:  [What problem are we solving better than alternatives?]
  Main competition:        [What do users use today instead?]
  Why us vs. them:         [Unique advantage in 10 words or less]

LAUNCH GOAL
  Primary metric:          [e.g., 500 downloads in first 30 days]
  Secondary metric:        [e.g., 4.5★ store rating at 30-day mark]
  Anti-goal:               [e.g., NOT chasing large installs with low retention]

LAUNCH TYPE:
  [ ] Soft launch (limited geography or audience — for data before scaling)
  [ ] Full public launch (all markets at once)
  [ ] Staged rollout (Android: 10% → 50% → 100%)
  [ ] Invite-only / waitlist (controlled access)

BUDGET: [Paid marketing budget, if any: $____]
```

## Part 2: Launch Channels

Select and plan your launch channels based on your audience, budget, and resources.

### 2.1 Owned Channels (Free — use always)

```
OWNED CHANNEL PLAN

[ ] App store listing (ASO — see app-store-submission.md)
    Ensure: compelling screenshots, strong keywords, solid description

[ ] Creator's social media
    Platforms: [Instagram / Twitter/X / LinkedIn / TikTok / YouTube]
    Pre-launch posts: Tease the product [2 weeks before launch]
    Launch post: [Day 0]
    Post-launch: [Weekly updates for 4 weeks]

[ ] Newsletter / email list (if you have one)
    Audience size: [N subscribers]
    Pre-launch: Build anticipation — [2-3 emails in 2 weeks before]
    Launch email: [Day 0 — clear CTA to download]
    Post-launch: [Follow-up with early user stories, features]

[ ] Website / landing page
    URL: [yourdomain.com/app]
    Includes: Screenshots, key benefits, download links, email capture
    Launch: Available at least 1 week before app launch
    [ ] Landing page live and tested: [Date]

[ ] Product Hunt (free launch platform, good for tech audience)
    [ ] Create product on Product Hunt
    [ ] Schedule launch for [Date] — Tuesday/Wednesday 12:01am PT is peak
    [ ] Line up "hunters" (influential PH members who post the product)
    [ ] Prepare all PH assets: logo, description, screenshots, links
    [ ] Brief supporters to upvote on launch day
```

### 2.2 Earned Channels (Free — but requires effort)

```
EARNED CHANNEL PLAN

[ ] App store reviews (seed before launch)
    Strategy: Ask beta testers who loved it to leave a review on day 1
    In-app review prompt: Implement using StoreKit (iOS) or Play Core (Android)
    Do NOT incentivize reviews — policy violation

[ ] Press / media outreach
    Target outlets:   [List 5-10 relevant publications or blogs]
    Pitch angle:      [Why is this story interesting to THEIR readers? Be specific]
    Timing:           Send embargoed pitches [1 week before launch]
    Press kit:        [Prepare: screenshots, icon, founder photo, one-paragraph description]

[ ] Community engagement
    Communities:      [Reddit: r/___; Discord: ___; Facebook Group: ___; Forum: ___]
    Strategy:         Engage authentically first — don't just post. Be a contributor.
    Share-worthy:     Create genuinely useful post (not just "I made an app")
    Timing:           Start engaging [3-4 weeks before launch]; reveal [Day 0]

[ ] Influencer / creator outreach (micro-influencers in niche)
    Target:           [5-10 micro-influencers with engaged niche audiences]
    Ask:              Honest review or mention — no fake endorsements
    Compensation:     Lifetime access / premium subscription / small fee
    Lead time:        Start outreach [4-6 weeks before launch]

[ ] Content marketing (builds long-term organic discovery)
    Format:           [Blog posts / YouTube videos / TikTok / Twitter threads]
    Topic angle:      Share knowledge related to the problem your app solves — not just about the app
    Examples:
      "How I learned [skill] in 30 days" → reveals app is how you did it
      "The 5 tools I use for [workflow]" → your app is one of them
    Timeline:         Publish 1-2 pieces per week starting [4 weeks before launch]
```

### 2.3 Paid Channels (Budget required — use if budget available)

```
PAID CHANNEL PLAN   [Budget: $____]

[ ] App Store Search Ads (Apple Search Ads)
    Focus: High-intent search terms in the App Store
    Budget: $5-20/day minimum for meaningful data
    Campaign: Basic campaign targeting your category keywords
    Measure: Cost per Install (CPI) — target varies by app

[ ] Google UAC (Universal App Campaigns)
    Focus: Discovery across Google Search, Play, YouTube, Display
    Budget: $20-50/day minimum for learning phase
    Assets needed: Text ads, images, video (60 sec)

[ ] Meta Ads (Instagram + Facebook)
    Focus: Interest-based targeting matching your user profile
    Budget: $15-30/day minimum per experiment
    Creative: Video ads outperform static for app install campaigns
    Audience: [Describe the interest/demographic targeting]

[ ] Social media sponsored posts
    Target: Influencer partnerships with sponsored disclosure

Note: Paid channels need 7-14 days of data before optimizing. Don't kill campaigns early.
```

## Part 3: Launch Timeline

```
LAUNCH TIMELINE — [Project Name]

TARGET LAUNCH DATE: [YYYY-MM-DD]

T-30 DAYS (One month out)
  [ ] Content marketing pieces in production — first 2 pieces published
  [ ] Landing page live
  [ ] Press kit prepared
  [ ] Influencer/creator outreach begun
  [ ] Beta program actively running

T-14 DAYS
  [ ] App submitted to App Store / Google Play
  [ ] Product Hunt listing created (not yet launched)
  [ ] Social media posts drafted and scheduled
  [ ] Press pitches sent (embargoed to launch day)
  [ ] Beta closed — final feedback incorporated
  [ ] Marketing screenshots uploaded to stores

T-7 DAYS
  [ ] App store review received — app approved
  [ ] Pre-launch social media teaser posts begin
  [ ] Email to newsletter (early access / launch date announcement)
  [ ] Coordinate with supporters for Product Hunt launch
  [ ] Community warm-up posts (not launching yet — just sharing progress)

T-1 DAY (Day Before)
  [ ] Final check: Download the app fresh install — everything works
  [ ] Social media posts for tomorrow scheduled
  [ ] Email for tomorrow drafted and ready to send
  [ ] Product Hunt launch time set
  [ ] Team/friends briefed: "Tomorrow is launch day — please support!"
  [ ] Store listing live (even if app not yet available — pre-order if possible)

DAY 0 — LAUNCH DAY
  [ ] App set to Available (or confirm store approved it)
  [ ] Product Hunt posted (if applicable) — 12:01am PT
  [ ] Launch social media post(s) published at [scheduled time]
  [ ] Launch email sent to newsletter
  [ ] Reply to all early comments, reviews, messages — be responsive
  [ ] Monitor crash reports — be ready to release a hotfix within hours
  [ ] Track downloads / installs in real time

T+7 DAYS
  [ ] Second wave marketing: share first numbers, user stories, or user quote
  [ ] Reply to all app store reviews (good and bad)
  [ ] Product Hunt follow-up post (if launched there)
  [ ] Review crash reports — release patch if needed

T+14 DAYS
  [ ] First in-app review prompt enabled (for users who reached "aha moment")
  [ ] Review analytics: what's the drop-off? Where do users churn?
  [ ] Community follow-up: "We launched! Here's what we learned..."
  [ ] Evaluate paid channels if budget available

T+30 DAYS
  [ ] Review launch metrics vs. GTM goals
  [ ] Retrospective (see post-launch/retrospective.md)
  [ ] Plan first feature update based on user feedback
  [ ] Gate 7 check-in: Continue growing? Pivot? Sunset?
```

## Tier Adjustments

| Aspect | Lite | Standard | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GTM channels** | 1-2 owned channels | 3-5 multi-channel plan | Full multi-channel + PR + paid |
| **Budget** | $0 (organic only) | $0 to $500/month | $500-$5,000+/month |
| **Content marketing** | Optional | 1 piece per launch | Ongoing content calendar |
| **PR outreach** | No | DIY 3-5 outlets | PR agency or specialist |
| **Product Hunt** | Optional | Recommended | Coordinated launch |
| **Metrics tracking** | Monthly | Weekly | Daily dashboard |
| **Post-launch iteration** | Quarterly | Monthly | 2-week sprint cycles |
